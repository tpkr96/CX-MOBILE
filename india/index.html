Dhan Cloud version

The full bot (both engines, NSE + BSE, cash + F&O, Super Orders with trailing stop) runs on Dhan Cloud as Python. Paste main.py and requirements.txt into cloud.dhanhq.co/editor and schedule weekdays 09:10–15:35 IST. It is paper-only too.

Connect to Dhan (live prices)

Prices come from Dhan's live market feed over WebSocket. Your access token is kept only in this tab's memory: it is never saved on the phone or sent anywhere except Dhan. Get it from web.dhan.co → My Profile → DhanHQ Trading APIs (valid 24 hours). The Data API subscription must be active.

Paper settings

Daily loss stop 3% and kill switch 20% are fixed. Intraday positions close at 15:15 IST.
